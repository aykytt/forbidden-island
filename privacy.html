Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no login, no registration and no user profiles. We operate no servers of our own and receive no information about you or about how you use the app.

What Is Stored On Your Device

So that you do not have to set up your lights again every time, the app saves two values in local storage on your device:

  • The local network address of your Philips Hue Bridge (for example 192.168.1.20)
  • The access token your Bridge issues to the app when you press the button on the Bridge

Both values stay on your device. They are never sent to us or to any third party. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

Your Philips Hue Bridge

All lighting commands travel directly from your device to your Hue Bridge over your own local network. They do not pass through us and they do not leave your home network.

Requests The App Makes To The Internet

The app does contact three external services. We do not send them anything about you, your gameplay, your lights or the contents of your device. As with any internet request, though, the service being contacted can see the public IP address the request comes from. For full transparency, this is the complete list:

  • Philips Hue discovery service (discovery.meethue.com, operated by Signify) — used only for the optional automatic Bridge search, to look up the local address of a Bridge on your network. It runs when you tap Connect. If you type your Bridge address in manually, it is not used at all.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) — loads the typeface used in the interface.
  • jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net) — loads the stylesheet used for the interface layout.

The font and stylesheet requests are made by the interface when the app opens. Sound effects and music are bundled inside the app and are never downloaded.

Third-Party Services We Do Not Use

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash or performance reporting, no tracking technologies and no social media integrations. There are no in-app purchases and no payment processing of any kind.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect data from any user, including children under the age of 13. It is not directed at children and it contains no advertising and no purchasing.

Your Rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct, export or delete on request. Everything the app stores is on your own device and under your control. If you have a question about this, write to us at the address above.
